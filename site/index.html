Method

Separate records from interpretations

Each layer has a deliberately narrow job. The framework preserves exact source material and asks analytical statements to show their evidence and uncertainty.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Store a bounded, immutable Observation with provenance.

  2. 02

    Isolate

    Expose exact passages, source context, and visible actor aliases.

  3. 03

    Annotate

    Optionally mark visible language patterns with local RhetorLint.

  4. 04

    Assess

    Link scoped claims and falsifiable hypotheses to cited passages.

Epistemic boundaries

Validation is structure, not a truth machine

A valid packet can be traced and reviewed. That does not make its contents correct.

What the framework can preserve

  • Exact visible passages and source-record lineage
  • Claimed source ID and canonical-host consistency
  • Bounded, potentially incomplete actor-alias visibility
  • Evidence links, alternatives, limitations, and falsifiers

What it does not establish

  • Truth, deception, source credibility, or authentication
  • Independent corroboration from publisher diversity
  • Power from mention volume or capacity from identity alone
  • Intent from rhetoric, capability, or automated inference
Intent belongs here only as a falsifiable, evidence-linked hypothesis with alternatives—not as mind-reading or a confidence score.
